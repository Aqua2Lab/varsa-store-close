How to use this page
  1. Choose the year, the month, and the week. Use Previous and Next to move as far ahead or back as you need.
  2. On the week, pick a cashier for every shift. On-call 1 and On-call 2 are extra people. Leave them blank when you do not need them.
  3. Under the grids, set the manager’s From and To times. That time is salary and stays out of the labor budget.
  4. Read the budget at the top. Amber means the store is over the hours you budgeted.
  5. Press Save this week. Then use “Use this week for more dates” or “Copy a month” when you want the same pattern again.
  6. Month, Year, and Scheduled list show what is already saved.
