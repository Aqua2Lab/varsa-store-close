How to read the report (Store #299 Chevron)
  1. Fuel: copy Total Fuel Sales and Fuel Discounts from the fuel section. Enter the discount as a positive number.
  2. Departments (Beer, Cigarettes, Novelty, Oil, Tobacco, Vape): in the department sales section, find each name and copy its Net Sales number.
  3. Grocery group (Ice, TBA, Candy, Chips, Deli, Dept 1, Drinks, Grocery): same section, same Net Sales column. Any department not listed on this form goes in Grocery. Use the calculator to add them.
  4. Sales Tax: copy Total Taxes Collected.
  5. Total Sales: copy the total sales figure printed on the report.
  6. Credit/Debit: copy Network Revenue, which is the Ruby C/C total.
  7. Outgoing: these aren't on the report. Enter the bank deposit (cash minus the $300 drawer float), plus any FD130, coupons, cash purchases, and cash expenses.

Leave a field at 0 or blank if it isn't on the report.
