Nightly Store Close

Varsa Group LLC · fill from the Ruby2 Store Close report · goes to the Store Close Log

Logging steps (read once)
  1. Print the Store Close report (Chevron prints 3 pages; Outrun prints a long receipt).
  2. Fold the receipt in half twice so page 1's bottom lines (fuel sales, fuel discount, sales tax) sit next to the top.
  3. Pick your store below. The help under each box changes to your store's report.
  4. Fill the boxes top to bottom. A green ✓ means the box is filled. Press Enter / Next to jump to the next box.
  5. Count the cash. Leave $300 in the till; the rest is the bank deposit.
  6. Press Submit, then write "Logged" on the report and keep it with the deposit.

Thomasville Chevron · how to read the report

Store #299 report (3 pages)

  1. Total sales: Grand Total store sales reading, first line, page 1 of 3.
  2. Fuel sales: under Grand Total, the dollar value on the right side.
  3. Fuel discount: line just below fuel sales.
  4. Sales tax: Total Taxes Collected, just below fuel sales.
  5. Credit/Debit: Network Revenue, page 2 of 3. Credit + debit are already combined; no adding.
  6. Departments: Net Sales column for each department.

Thomasville Outrun · how to read the report

Store #167 report (long receipt, fold in half twice)

  1. Total sales: Net Sales Total under the All Registers summary, page 1.
  2. Fuel sales: bottom of page 1 (after folding twice).
  3. Fuel discount: the line just above fuel sales.
  4. Sales tax: bottom of page 1.
  5. Credit/Debit: under All Registers, add the separate Credit line and Debit line (type it as 500.25+300.10).
  6. Departments: Net Sales for each department.
